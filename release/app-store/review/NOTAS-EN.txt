AtendeBem 1.3.0 (40): native registration, adaptive welcome/login, revocable saved general-text AI consent, avatars, paged reports and Today shortcuts before the next appointment. Device/service QA, review account, media, Archive/IPA40 and upload are unconfirmed.

Native SwiftUI for healthcare professionals and clinic teams. Clinical use requires an active account, authorized clinic membership and internet. Existing members sign in; clinic owners can register natively. The app uses the same services as AtendeBem web, without changing the web, backend or database. Tabs: Hoje, Agenda, Pacientes and Mais.

Registration reads current eligible trial conditions from the public catalog before plan selection. Nine observed plans offer 14 days without a card; Rede does not. Account creation is free, with no purchases or external checkout. Lost responses are not automatically retried. Seven professional categories identify registrants, not complete specialty modules. No medication sales, dosage calculator or HealthKit.

Proposed review flow; credentials and the synthetic-data clinic still require preparation and validation:
1. Sign in with review credentials entered directly in the App Review fields. Never use real patient data.
2. Open Hoje: use the independent shortcuts, inspect the next appointment and resume an unfinished consultation. Unsaved local notes last only for the current app session.
3. In LARI, open or type a scheduling task; select the patient, professional, type and slot, then review before confirming. Lost responses require reconciliation, not automatic resubmission.
4. Request patient history and inspect the selected record, allergies, sources and partial coverage. Factual lookup does not automatically request an AI summary.
5. Review an exam request. The service attempts signing during creation; sending is separate after checking signature and contacts. Queued is not delivered. Prescription signing requests delivery through the clinic's configured channels.
6. For transcription, authorize participants and Google Gemini processing, then microphone access. Stopping recording does not send. Explicitly send, edit and review before applying to the current consultation draft. Nothing is automatically saved or signed.
7. Authorized roles can read finance, seasonality and municipal indicators. Reports paginate actual agenda intervals eight at a time, with exact values and missing/inconsistent-data states. Municipal figures are not patient demographics.
8. Drug lookup provides catalog records and the official leaflet search, not full leaflet ingestion. Interaction lookup requires reviewed ingredients and shows sources/dates from a limited database; no alert does not establish safety.
9. General-text LARI consent identifies Anthropic and Gemini and is stored by user/clinic/device and policy version. Revoke in Settings; open windows and send guards reflect the change. SOAP, patient data and audio require separate authorization. Revocation cannot erase messages already received by the service.
10. Test team chat/community with test users. Avatars use authenticated media; anonymous posts show neither author photo nor initials. PDF/app-link sharing uses the system sheet. Mais > Techbem links to the separate Meu Prontuário app; this does not connect accounts.
11. Check welcome/registration, clinic switching, privacy/support and accessibility. Account deletion starts at Mais > Configurações > Minha conta > Excluir minha conta; DPO operation and QA remain pending.
